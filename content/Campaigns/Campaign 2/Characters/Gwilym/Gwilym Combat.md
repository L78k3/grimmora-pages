> [!shield] Arcane Ward
> Whenever you cast an abjuration spell of 1st level or higher (Mage Armor, Shield, Alarm), you create/replenish a magical ward on yourself.
> **Ward HP:** 7 (2 × Wizard Level + INT modifier). 
> It takes damage instead of you. If reduced to 0, you take the remaining damage. Regains HP equal to twice the spell's level when subsequent Abjuration spells are cast.
> [!warning] Shield (1st Level Spell)
> When you are hit by an attack or targeted by the magic missile spell, use your **Reaction** to gain a +5 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn (taking your AC to 20 if Mage Armor is active).

## 🧬 Magic Reference (The "Protest Medic" Toolkit)
* **Spell Attack Bonus:** +5
* **Spell Save DC:** 13
* **Magic Missile (1st Level):** Create three glowing darts. Each hits a creature of your choice automatically for `dice: 1d4+1` force damage.
* **Mage Armor (1st Level):** Base AC becomes 13 + DEX modifier (15 total). Lasts 8 hours.
* **Sleep (1st Level):** Roll `dice: 5d8`; put that many hit points worth of creatures to sleep. No saving throw. Non-lethal crowd control.
* **Alarm (1st Level - Abjuration):** Set an alarm on a 20-foot cube area against unwanted intrusion. Triggers a mental ping or audible bell.
* **Minor Illusion (Cantrip):** Create a sound or an image of an object within 30 feet. Used to hide basement doors or distract guards during a sweep.
* **Message (Cantrip):** Point your finger and whisper a message to a target within 120 feet. Used to quietly tip off worshippers of approaching danger.
* **Mage Hand (Cantrip):** A spectral, floating hand that can manipulate objects up to 30 feet away.
* **Ritual Caster:** Can cast *Detect Magic*, *Find Familiar* (Brick), *Identify*, and *Alarm* without using spell slots if an extra 10 minutes is taken.
