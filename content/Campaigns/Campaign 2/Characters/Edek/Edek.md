# Edek Firehelm

> [!info] Character Overview
> **Race:** Hill Dwarf
> **Class:** Barbarian 2
> **Background:** Soldier
> **Alignment:** Neutral
> **Player:** Suzanne

## Biography & Personality
Edek is a 40-year-old Hill Dwarf standing 4'2" and weighing 169 lbs. She has olive skin, green eyes, and brown hair. She enjoys being strong, breaking things, and boasts that she can stare down a hell hound without flinching. Despite her fierce exterior, she operates under a philosophy of "Live and Let Live," believing ideals aren't worth killing over, though she maintains a personal code to fight for those who cannot fight for themselves. She has little respect for anyone who is not a proven warrior.

## Military Background
Edek's background as a Soldier grants her a recognized military rank. Soldiers loyal to her former organization defer to her authority, allowing her to requisition simple equipment, horses, and access to friendly military fortresses. She carries an insignia of rank and a trophy taken from a fallen enemy. 

## Allies & Organizations
* **The Triumvirate:** Edek has listed The Triumvirate as an allied organization.
