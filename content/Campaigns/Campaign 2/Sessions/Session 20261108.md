# Short summary
The party traveled from Hjemmen to Daggatt, repelling a goblin ambush on the road. Upon arriving, they discovered Daggatt completely slaughtered. After clearing scavengers, the party rescued a small group of survivors from the jail cells beneath the governor's mansion. While en route to investigate the darkened lighthouse, Brick spotted two brown bears breaking into Master Drev's isolated tower. The party defeated the beasts, only to find the cantankerous wizard unconscious inside. 

# Play-by-play
- **The Road Ambush:** Defeated a squad of goblins on the road from Hjemmen.
- **The Scavengers:** Defeated a group of hyenas and two vultures picking at the ruins of Daggatt.
- **The Tower Breach:** Intercepted and killed two brown bears actively breaking down the door to Drev's tower.
- **Roleplay (Jasper):** Gwilym tested the waters by offering to swap restricted texts for holy books.
- **Roleplay (Survivor):** A mother hiding with her two children in the jail was praying to the Tempest Gods for her sailor husband's return. Directed her to the next town.
- **Next Steps:** Jasper will stabilize Drev. Gwilym will slip away to search Drev's private desk for spell scrolls, predecessor info, Triumvirate research, and forgery seals.
