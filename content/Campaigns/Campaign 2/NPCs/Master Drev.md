**Master Drev** is an irascible Abjuration Wizard who 'rescued' Gwilym from the Inquisition.

## Biography & Personality
Drev is a cantankerous and demanding master who constantly compares Gwilym to his previous, deceased apprentice. He is permitted by the fanatical Bishop Greggan to study magic because his discoveries are too valuable to the Triumvirate to risk losing. He was recently found unconscious in his tower after a bear attack.

## Key Relationships
* **[[Gwilym]]:** His current apprentice, whom he treats as a compliant, by-the-book stooge.
* **[[The Predecessor]]:** His former apprentice who died under suspicious circumstances. 
* **[[Bishop Greggan]]:** Grants Drev immunity from the Inquisition in exchange for his research.
